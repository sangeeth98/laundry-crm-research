## 2. Store Unit Economics (FOFO Model Benchmark)

A standard Tier-1/2 Indian laundromat franchise operates under the following typical economics:

- **Initial Capex Setup**: ₹20L – ₹30L (Commercial washer-extractors, dryers, boiler, steam iron tables, POS counter, interior branding).
- **Monthly Gross Revenue**: ₹2.5L – ₹4.5L per store (~150–250 pieces/day average volume).
- **Direct Operating Expenses**:
  - Store Rent: ₹35K – ₹60K/mo (400–600 sq ft high-street retail).
  - Electricity & Water: ₹25K – ₹40K/mo.
  - Detergents & Chemicals: ₹15K – ₹25K/mo (Seitz, Diversey, or proprietary).
  - Staff Labor: ₹35K – ₹50K/mo (1 store manager + 2 ironing/washing technicians).
  - Franchise Royalty & Software Fee: 7% – 10% of gross revenue.
- **Store Payback Horizon**: 18 – 24 months to full initial capital recovery.

## 3. Operational Friction & Core Pain Points

Empirical analysis across customer reviews, Reddit forums, and store operator interviews revealed 5 systemic bottlenecks:

1. **Garment Loss & Misplacement**: Stapled paper tags detach inside wash drums; manual ticket numbering results in mixed-up customer bundles during festival peaks.
2. **Pre-Existing Defect Disputes**: Absence of photo-logging at counter intake leads to disputes over pre-existing tears, color bleeds, or fabric damage.
3. **Franchise Royalty Leakage**: Store franchisees hide cash walk-in transactions to evade corporate royalty deductions due to unintegrated offline POS terminals.
4. **Machine Idle Time & Misuse**: Unmonitored wash cycles run by store staff after-hours without billing entries.
5. **Turnaround & Delivery Delays**: Lack of route clustering and automated customer notifications for driver pickup and drop-off.
