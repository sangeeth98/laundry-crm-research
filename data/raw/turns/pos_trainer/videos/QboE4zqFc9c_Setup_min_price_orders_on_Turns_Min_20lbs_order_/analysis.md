## Feature Overview & Description
Laundromats and cleaners love to service their customers with the most innovative pricing schemes.
One of the most common pricing structure for a laundromat or cleaner is based on min. price or min.order.
Ex. Min 20lbs for pickup, Min 15 lbs for WDF
or 
Atleast $25 worth of pants to be cleaned
