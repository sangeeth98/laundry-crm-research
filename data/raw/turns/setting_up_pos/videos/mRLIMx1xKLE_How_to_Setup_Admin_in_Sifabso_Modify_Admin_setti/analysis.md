## Feature Overview & Description
This video is a short tutorial in a series of small playlists on how to setup your Sifabso Platform best suited for your Laundry and Dryclean business . In this video we will explain
 How to Reach to the Admin Settings
 Who can reach the admin settings 
How to modify the details of an Admin 
How to change the password for an Admin account

In the next few videos we would explain the permissions of an Admin and the features that the admin account holder controls on their business presentation and Website.
