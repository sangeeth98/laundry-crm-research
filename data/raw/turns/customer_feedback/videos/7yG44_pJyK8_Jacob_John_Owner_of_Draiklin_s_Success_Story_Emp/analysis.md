## Feature Overview & Description
Join Jacob John, Owner of Draiklin, as he shares the transformative journey of their dry cleaning and laundry business based in the vibrant city of Bangalore, India. With a strong IT background and corporate experience, Jacob and his team embarked on a mission to revolutionize the dry cleaning industry, recognizing its immense potential both locally and globally. Starting with a single, Draiklin aims to expand its footprint across India.
In this insightful video, Jacob delves into the critical decisions and challenges faced while scaling their operations. He highlights how selecting the right Point of Sale (POS) system was a game-changer, leading them to choose Turns. Discover how Turns not only addressed their operational pain points but also significantly improved efficiency, customer connectivity, and business promotion. From real-time updates to advanced reporting and marketing tools, learn how Turns has become an integral part of Draiklin's success.
Jacob also emphasizes the ease and comfort of using Turns for his team, thanks to its user-friendly interface and multilingual support. Witness how Turns helped Draiklin increase customer retention, drive growth, and achieve a remarkable increase in revenue through its innovative features.
Tune in to get inspired by Draiklin's journey and learn how technology like Turns can elevate your business to new heights. Whether you're in the dry cleaning industry or any other service sector, this story is a testament to the power of innovation, determination, and the right technological tools.
