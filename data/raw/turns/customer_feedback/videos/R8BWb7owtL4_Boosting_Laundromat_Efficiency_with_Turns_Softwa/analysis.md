## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to our review of Turns, the game-changing laundromat software that has transformed our operations at The Wash House in Newburgh, New York. Say goodbye to manual note-taking and piles of paper! With Turns, our laundry business has become streamlined and efficient.

Gone are the days of worrying about lost slips. Turns automates the process by sending text messages to our customers, ensuring they have peace of mind and no longer fret about misplaced receipts. The system stores all customer information, allowing us to easily send updates and exciting offers through text messages.

Thanks to the seamless integration with Doordash, we now offer convenient pickup and delivery services to our valued customers. The software sends timely notifications, keeping both us and our customers informed every step of the way.

Inventory management has become a breeze with Turns. It provides accurate analysis of our orders and sales, allowing us to make informed decisions. We can effortlessly compare our products with others in the market, track sales and costs more effectively, and even monitor peak customer visiting times.

The time and energy saved using Turns are immeasurable. Our worries have been eliminated, and our Laundromat business now operates at optimal efficiency. We highly recommend Turns to any laundry business looking to simplify operations and take their business to new heights.
