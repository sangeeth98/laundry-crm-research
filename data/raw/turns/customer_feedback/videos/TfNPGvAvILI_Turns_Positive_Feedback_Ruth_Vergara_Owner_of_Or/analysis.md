## Feature Overview & Description
Ruth Vergara, the owner of Orchard Laundromat, provided a positive video testimonial about Turns Laundromat POS System. When asked about the specific issues she faced when starting her Laundromat, Ruth mentioned that her employees were not fully bilingual, and this posed a challenge for her as Spanish was her first language. However, Turns' Spanish menu made the transition easier for her employees, simplifying their tasks such as printing receipts.

Ruth shared that customers were delighted with the POS system. The ability to print out receipts instead of using handwritten ones was a game-changer. Additionally, the automated text notifications to customers when their orders were ready eliminated the need for manual calls and greatly pleased customers.

Regarding customer support, Ruth expressed her satisfaction with Turns' support system, praising their helpfulness. While she acknowledged that there is always room for improvement, she had no complaints after using the system for a few months and would recommend it to others due to its user-friendly nature.

When comparing Turns to other platforms, Ruth highlighted the simplicity of Turns' system, which made it easy for customers to use. She also appreciated the integration with Doordash, allowing her to book delivery services through the platform. This feature was particularly valuable when her delivery person was unavailable.

In her overall feedback, Ruth described Turns as amazing and expressed gratitude for their partnership. As a female owner in the field, she found Turns to be a great support system, making her life easier. She also emphasized the convenience of being able to access all the laundromat's stats remotely from her office, making Turns a valuable tool for managing her business.

In conclusion, Ruth Vergara's review of Turns Laundromat POS System highlighted the platform's effectiveness in overcoming language barriers, improving customer satisfaction, providing excellent customer support, simplicity of use, integration with Doordash, and the overall convenience it brought to her business operations.
