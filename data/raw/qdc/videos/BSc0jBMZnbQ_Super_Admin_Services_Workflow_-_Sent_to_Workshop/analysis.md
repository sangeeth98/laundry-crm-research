## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin: Services Workflow - Sent to Workshop, in this training video we will understand the followings:

- How can I track garments sent for processing to a vendor?
- How do I control different stages of garments through services workflow for garment tracking purposes?
