## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin: Services Workflow - Disable Garment Stages, in this training video we will understand the followings:

- I don’t want to track garments at multiple stages.
- How can I disable garment stages?
- How can I mark garments ready automatically for a service/ all services?
