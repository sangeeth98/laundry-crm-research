## Feature Overview & Description
In this video we will under stand the new update for Saudi Arabia. This applies once the time zone is updated in CRM. This ensures seamless adherence to local tax requirements.
All invoices now include a Quick Response (QR) code as mandated by ZATCA regulations.

PS: The QR codes are fully compatible with the official ZATCA app, allowing quick and secure access to invoice details.
Compliance with Phase II e-Invoicing: This feature is designed to meet the requirements of ZATCA's e-invoicing Phase II rollout, effective from January 1, 2023.
