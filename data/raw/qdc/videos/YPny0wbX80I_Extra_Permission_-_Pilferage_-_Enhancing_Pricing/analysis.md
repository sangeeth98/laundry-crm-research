## Feature Overview & Description
You can now prevent staff from decreasing garment prices while creating orders. This ensures that your business stays protected from unauthorized discounts and revenue leakages.

Key Benefits:
•⁠  ⁠Stops leakages at the staff end
•⁠  ⁠Prevents unnecessary lower prices for preferred customers
•⁠  ⁠Gives you total control over your business pricing
•⁠  ⁠Applies to both Laundry and Dry Cleaning orders
