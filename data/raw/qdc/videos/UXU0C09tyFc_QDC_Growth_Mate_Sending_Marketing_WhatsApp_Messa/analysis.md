## Feature Overview & Description
In QDC, you can now directly send campaigns through WhatsApp based on filtered customer data without exporting to Excel.
This enhancement ensures customer data privacy and security while sending campaign

PS- This is a paid feature: Requires Growth Mate - Promote (Add-on)
