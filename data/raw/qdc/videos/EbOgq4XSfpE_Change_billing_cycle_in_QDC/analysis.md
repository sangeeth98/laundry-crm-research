## Feature Overview & Description
In this video we will understand how you can change the billing cycle of your subscription to yearly and vice versa. QDC gives you the convenient option to pay monthly or yearly. The yearly billing cycle gives you the savings of 20% and the monthly billing gives you the feasibility to pay in smaller amounts. Let us understand how you can do the same

-How to check your billing cycle
-How to change the billing cycle from monthly to yearly or vice versa
-How the amount is calculated on pro rata basis
-What is the price difference in monthly and yearly billing
