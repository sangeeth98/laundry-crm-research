## Feature Overview & Description
With the recent Chrome update, you may need to adjust your browser settings to enable printing. Please follow the steps below or refer to the attached video for guidance:

Go to the login page of your software.

Click on the lock icon (🔒) on the left side of the address bar (as shown in the image).

Enable the “Local Network Access” permission if it’s disabled.

Reload the page.

Log in again and try to print.

Following these steps should resolve the issue. Please check and confirm after applying the changes.
