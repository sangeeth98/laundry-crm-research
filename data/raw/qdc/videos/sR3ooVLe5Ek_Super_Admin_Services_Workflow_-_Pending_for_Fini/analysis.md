## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin: Services Workflow - Pending for Finishing, in this training video we will understand the followings:

- What is pending for the finishing stage, and how it helps?
- I want to enable a single garment tracking stage.
- I do pressing/ ironing in the store, How do I track garments in the ironing department?
