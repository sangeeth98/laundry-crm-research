## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin - User Management: Understanding Pre-configured Roles and Permission in this training video we will understand the followings:

- How many default roles are available? 
- Why are there so many roles? What is the use of different roles?
- What are the default permissions assigned to various roles in the store CRM (Administrator, Store incharge, POS Operator, Manager, Finance, and Call Center)?
- What are the default permissions assigned to various roles in the workshop? (workshop admin, and operator)?
- What are the default permissions assigned to various roles in the mPOS?
- What are the default permissions assigned to various roles in the super admin?
