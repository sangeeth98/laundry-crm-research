## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin - Garment Category: Understand Garment Categories, in this training video we will understand the followings:

- What is the garment category?
- Why do we need a garment category?
- How to create a garment category
- What is the regional language option in the garment category?
- How to delete or mark a category inactive?
