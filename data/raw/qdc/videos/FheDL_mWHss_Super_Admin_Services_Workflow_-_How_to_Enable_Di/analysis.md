## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin: Services Workflow - How to Enable/Disable Discount on Services, in this training video we will understand the followings:

- I want to enable/disable discounts on service across all stores?
- I want to enable/disable discounts on service at selected stores?
- What is the difference between flat and percentage discounts?
