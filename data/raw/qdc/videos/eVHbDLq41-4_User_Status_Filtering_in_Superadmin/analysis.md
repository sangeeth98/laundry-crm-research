## Feature Overview & Description
We have introduced a new filtering feature in the Users & Permissions section of the SuperAdmin panel to enhance user management:

Active/Inactive Filter: Easily filter users based on their status (Active/Inactive).
Default Sorting: Active users are now displayed at the top, followed by Inactive users for better visibility and management.
