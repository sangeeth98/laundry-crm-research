## Feature Overview & Description
Welcome to the video tutorial of Super Admin: Services Workflow - Combination of Stages, in this training video we will understand the followings:

- I have a single store, however, I send some garments for processing to a vendor or workshop, AND some garments are processed in-store.

- I have multiple stores, and they have a different setup. Some stores have in-house processing units for certain services (laundry, ironing) and some stores send all garments for processing to the workshop/vendor. 

- How do I configure/set up service individually for different stores?

If you haven't subscribed to QDC yet, click here - https://www.quickdrycleaning.com/start-free-trial/
