## Feature Overview & Description
In the Home Pickup Scheduler when you click on the "Convert to Order" option, a popup will appear with two buttons:

Create Order Per Piece - This option is designed for dry cleaning orders, allowing you to create detailed orders based on individual items.

Create Order Per Weight - This option is  for laundry orders, enabling you to process orders based on the total weight of the items.

This feature ensures a seamless and efficient order creation process, catering to the specific needs of both dry cleaning and laundry services.
