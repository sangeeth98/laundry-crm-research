## Feature Overview & Description
The Centralized Customer Listing Screen simplifies customer management with powerful tools for searching, filtering, and organizing customer data.

It features fast loading with pagination, customizable columns, advanced sorting, and robust search and filter options. Users can save dynamic filters for segmentation, take bulk actions like blocking or moving customers, and send secure WhatsApp campaigns without exporting data.
