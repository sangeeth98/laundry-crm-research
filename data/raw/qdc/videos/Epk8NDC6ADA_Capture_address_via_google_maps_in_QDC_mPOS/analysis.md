## Feature Overview & Description
The new feature in mPOS allows for the capture of customer addresses during the creation of new customer profiles as well as when updating the addresses of existing customers. 
This integration uses Google Maps to accurately retrieve and input address details, ensuring precise location information. By capturing and updating customer addresses, this feature enhances the efficiency of order processing and delivery management.
