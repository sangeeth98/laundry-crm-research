## Feature Overview & Description
The Driver Assign Page now has a user-friendly capability to optimize route prioritization. With this enhancement, you can visually arrange pickup and drop-off tasks directly on a grid by simply dragging and dropping them using your mouse.

This intuitive interface allows you to set the priority of tasks in real time, ensuring that pickups and drop-offs are organized efficiently. By rearranging tasks on the grid, you can create the most optimal sequence for drivers, saving time and improving overall logistics management.
