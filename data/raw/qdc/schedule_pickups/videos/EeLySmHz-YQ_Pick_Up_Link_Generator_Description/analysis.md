## Feature Overview & Description
Effortless Pickup Scheduling via Website & Social Media!

Simplify Pickup Requests & Boost Your Customer Engagement

We’re excited to introduce an easy way for your customers to schedule pickups—directly from your website and social media! With our new “Schedule Pick Up” Button & Shareable Link, you can seamlessly integrate pickup scheduling into your digital presence.

What’s New?
Now, you have two powerful ways to offer pickup scheduling:

Custom Pickup Button for Websites – Create a fully branded pickup button from your Admin Panel, generate a script, and embed it on your website in just a few clicks.
Shareable Pickup Link for Social Media & Ads – No website? No problem! Generate a dedicated scheduling link and share it across Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Ads, YouTube, and other platforms.

Why This is a Game-Changer for Your Business
Frictionless Customer Experience – Customers can schedule pickups easily without extra steps.
Consistent Branding – A customizable button that matches your website’s design.
More Customer Reach – Accept pickup requests from social media, ads, and digital campaigns.
Zero Technical Hassle – No coding required—just embed the button or share the link.

Key Features & How It Works
Website Pickup Button
New “Pick Up Link Generator” Section in the Admin Panel.
Full Customization – Adjust button color, text, font, border radius, and title to match your brand.
One-Click Script Generation – Copy and embed the script to add a Schedule Pickup button to your website.

Social Media & External Link Integration
Unique Shareable Pickup Link – Generate and share it across Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Ads, and more.
One-Click Access – Customers tap the link, enter their details, and book a pickup instantly.
No Website Needed – Perfect for businesses running on social media!
