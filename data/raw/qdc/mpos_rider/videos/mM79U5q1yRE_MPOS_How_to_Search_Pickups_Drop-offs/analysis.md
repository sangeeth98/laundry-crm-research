## Feature Overview & Description
This tutorial explains how to search and manage assigned pickups and drop-offs using the MPOS Rider App.

You will learn how to view orders based on Priority or Distance, making it easier to plan your daily route efficiently. The video also demonstrates how to view today's, tomorrow's, and upcoming pickup and drop-off schedules.
