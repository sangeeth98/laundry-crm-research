## Feature Overview & Description
How to Assign a Rack in Swash Laundry Software (SLS)

Welcome to the official Swash Laundry Software (SLS) tutorial series.

In this video, you'll learn how to assign a rack in Swash Laundry Software step by step. Rack assignment helps organize processed garments, makes order tracking easier, and improves storage management.

In this video, you'll learn:
How to open the Assign Rack module
How to select an order or invoice
How to assign a rack location
How to save the rack assignment
Best practices for managing rack locations efficiently

Swash Laundry Software (SLS) is a complete laundry management solution that helps you manage customers, orders, billing, rack management, bag management, pickup & delivery, payments, reports, and much more.
