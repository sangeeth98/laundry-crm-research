## Feature Overview & Description
How to Manage Additional Charges in Swash Laundry Software (SLS)

Welcome to the official Swash Laundry Software (SLS) tutorial series.

In this video, you'll learn how to add and manage Additional Charges in Swash Laundry Software. This feature allows you to apply extra charges such as express service fees, delivery charges, stain treatment fees, special garment handling charges, and other service-related costs.

In this video, you'll learn:
How to open the Additional Charges module
How to create a new additional charge
How to edit or update existing charges
How to apply additional charges to customer orders
Best practices for managing extra service charges
