## Feature Overview & Description
Managing multiple laundry outlets is no small task — but with SLS (Swash Laundry Software), it's organized, automated, and scalable.

In this video, hear from a successful franchise owner who uses the SLS Franchise Module to streamline operations across multiple branches.

🔑 Key Benefits Highlighted:
• Centralized control of multiple stores
• Individual store performance tracking
• Role-based staff access and permissions
• Unified billing, reports, and customer data
• Seamless integration with mobile app for pickup & delivery

Whether you're a single-store owner or running a chain, this video shows how SLS is built to grow with your business.
