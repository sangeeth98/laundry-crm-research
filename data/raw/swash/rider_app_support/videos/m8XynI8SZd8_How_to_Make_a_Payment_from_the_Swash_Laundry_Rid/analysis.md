## Feature Overview & Description
How to Make a Payment from the Swash Laundry Rider App

Welcome to the official **Swash Laundry Software (SLS)** tutorial series.

In this video, you'll learn how to collect and record customer payments using the **Swash Laundry Rider App**. This feature enables riders to accept payments during pickup or delivery and update the payment status instantly.

### In this video, you'll learn:

* How to log in to the Rider App
* How to open a customer order
* How to collect a customer payment
* How to select the payment method
* How to confirm and save the payment
* How to verify the updated payment status

Using the **Swash Laundry Rider App**, riders can efficiently manage pickups, deliveries, order updates, and payment collections in real time, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free customer experience.
