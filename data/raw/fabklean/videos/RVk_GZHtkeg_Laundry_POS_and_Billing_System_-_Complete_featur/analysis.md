## Feature Overview & Description
https://fabklean.com

http://fabklean.com/features-fabklean-laundry-business-software.html

Fabklean is a cloud based solution for laundry & dry cleaning business stores. Business can be a single or multi store, Fabklean supports end to end automation with out any issues.

Fabklean comes best POS system, store administrator module, work area module, plant operations support, mobile apps for drivers, customers, and owners. Also, gives deep drive insights to give complete picture about the business. 

With Fabklean customer facing mobile app, customers can download, raise pickup request at their convenient time. Customer even track status of order when its in process. Customer can raise complaint, check the loyalty points, buy the services online with payment over mobile phone, and check the store location etc.

Platform has world class POS system built into it. As a front end store operator (or) driver at customer location, can quickly enter order with touch based terminals. They can quickly apply the discounts, find out customer profile, easily apply price-list based on customer profile, check for previous dues, pitch about packages they are offer, and immediately apply them if customer is interested, capture the properties of the garments - color, damage, fabric, pattern, extra price etc, so that there wont be any disputes at later point of time.  Front end user can set the delivery dates (automatically comes form platform), driver details if the order is pickup, and capture the payments at order time itself.

Fabklean comes with Plant operations if the business has multiple stores. All the garment movement across the stores, delivery challan prints, plant to store communication, assembling & un-assembling of garments with garment tag printing etc. 

Vendor management, Purchase Order capability, and inventory tracking are other critical functionality that comes with the platform. As owner of multi store business, you can check the inventory levels, usage patterns, wastage of materials etc from the single pane of glass.  You can transfer the inventory from one store to other, capture adjustment reasons if inventory levels mis match when periodic checks made

Financial data empower owner with better control on the cash flow. Owner can login to the mobile app/portal check the financial health of all the stores without logging-in multiple times.  Application gives the visibility of all the dues, and can send the reminders to the customers, and try to get the payments on time. It gives the easy payment links to the customers to pay over online quickly. Notifications will be sent to the stake holders upon payment settlements.

Platform provides loyalty & reward point capability to the business owners. Business owner can decide reward point policy, define it for better customer engagement. Loyalty & reward points are incremented based on the customer usage, and stickiness. Business owner can also send the campaigning over the policy changes, and reward point thresholds.

CRM was built with platform. Business owner can create different campaigns over the events, festivals, public functions, and send them to their customers and attract new customers. With proper usage of campaigns they can generate more revenue from the new as well as existing customers. Campaigns can be sent across - Email, WhatsApp and SMS. You can measure performance of campaigns based on discount coupons associated with that. 

Assembling & Un-assembling of clothes for washing is very critical for cost cutting and improving the profits.  Platform generate garment tag prints immediately after order is created, and staff can pin them to the clothes, before goto wash area. After wash, they will be assembled back into individual orders based barcode printed over the garment tag, and scanner will scan the code, mark it as washing is done.

Geo tagging, Geo tracking and Geo fencing are very critical for businesses providing the pickup & delivery services. Customer are geo-tagged with their location, and drivers use mobile app to find location for pickup & delivery needs. Application gives the shortest route map for today's pickup & deliveries.  Where as owner can geo track the drivers, when they are in field for pickups & deliveries. This empower the owner for better utilisation of drivers for live pickups, and he can know the expected delivery or pickup whenever a customer calls.
Customers use the mobile apps, to capture their location and tag it to pickup request,  check the driver location when drivers are coming for the pickups & deliveries.

Other leakage area in the store is - Expenses.  Platform gives ability to quickly feed expenses made by staff. Each staff user is given login, and they can login to the system either through web or mobile app, and can feed the expenses they spent.  Owner can get the insights over the month - where are they spending, how much, spend by etc.  It will be easy for the owner to do the reimbursements.
