# Tumbledry Solutions Pvt Ltd

## Overview
Tumbledry is the largest organized laundry and dry-cleaning brand in India by store count, operating over **1,500+ stores** across **600+ cities** in Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 locations.[^tumbledry-official]

## Key Details
- **Founders**: Gaurav Nigam, Navin Chawla, Gaurav Teotia
- **Inception Year**: 2019
- **Headquarters**: Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India
- **Business Model**: Franchise-Owned Franchise-Operated (FOFO)
- **Funding**: Bootstrapped (Initial ₹2.5 Cr founder equity)
- **Revenue**: ₹50 Cr – ₹100 Cr range (FY25)

## Technology & Operational Friction
- **CRM Tech**: Proprietary mobile application and POS store management portal.
- **Garment Tagging**: Barcode paper tagging.
- **Top Complaint**: High occurrence of misplaced/lost garments and fabric color bleeding.
