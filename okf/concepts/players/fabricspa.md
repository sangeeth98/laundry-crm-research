# Fabricspa (Jyothy Fabricare Services Ltd)

## Overview
Fabricspa operates centralized industrial processing plants serving major Indian metros (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi/NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Goa).

## Key Details
- **Promoter Parent**: Jyothy Labs Limited (M. P. Ramachandran)
- **Inception Year**: 2009
- **Headquarters**: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
- **Business Model**: Central Processing Unit (CPU) + Retail Hubs
- **Funding**: Corporate Parent Subsidiary
- **Estimated Revenue**: ₹40 Cr – ₹60 Cr

## Technology & Operational Friction
- **CRM Tech**: Acquired Quiclo platform POS, barcode manifests, centralized call center.
- **Top Complaint**: High pricing and slow turnaround due to transport lag from retail store to central processing plant.
